01 / The useful part

不是一个录音机，是一个输入层。Not a recorder. An input layer.

按一下开始，再按一下停止。OpenTypeless 负责剩下的事情：转录、格式化、插入。Press once to start, once to stop. OpenTypeless handles the rest: transcribe, format, insert.

01 — TOGGLE TO TALK

说话，不用盯着窗口Talk without watching a window

全局快捷键从菜单栏启动。录音状态和音量在屏幕底部轻量提示。Start from anywhere with a global shortcut. A small overlay shows state and audio level.

02 — SMART INSERTION

文字回到你正在写的地方Text returns to where you write

优先使用 Accessibility 插入；对 Terminal、cmux 等目标使用受保护的粘贴回退。Accessibility insertion first, with a guarded paste fallback for Terminal, cmux, and friends.

03 — YOUR PROVIDER

选择模型，也选择边界Choose the model and the boundary

03 / Privacy by default

你的声音，不经过我们。Your voice, not through us.

OpenTypeless 没有账号、广告、追踪或开发者运营的服务器。你选择的 provider 由应用直接调用，只有在你阅读并同意数据处理说明后才会发送。OpenTypeless has no accounts, ads, tracking, or developer-operated servers. The provider you choose is called directly from the app, only after you review and accept the data-processing disclosure.
